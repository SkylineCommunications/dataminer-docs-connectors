# Electrolink FM Transmitter 100W

## About

The **Electrolink FM Transmitter 100W** connector gives operators a consolidated view of transmitter operation through SNMP. Monitor RF output and reflected power, thermal and supply measurements, and key transmitter states in DataMiner, with alarming and trending for operational follow-up.

## Key Features

- Monitor overall transmitter state, forward and reflected power, heatsink temperature, driver power, and supply voltage and current.
- Track fan, interlock, VSWR, temperature, RF output, and VSWR block status.
- Enable or disable the transmitter and adjust its configured output power level remotely through the connector.
- Configure LAN settings, SNMP trap destination, and transmitter email notification settings.
- Adjust polling intervals and enable or disable individual datasets with the Polling Manager.

## Use Cases

- Review transmitter health and RF measurements from DataMiner, and use trends and alarms to support operational monitoring.
- Manage transmitter state and output power level from the element interface.
- Configure notification destinations and tune dataset polling to suit operational needs.

## Compatibility

The connector requires DataMiner 10.4.0.0 build 14003 or later. The available protocol and MIB sources do not specify transmitter firmware compatibility; supported firmware versions are therefore unspecified.
