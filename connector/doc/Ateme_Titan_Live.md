# Ateme Titan Live

## About

The Ateme Titan Live is a live video encoding platform that turns SDI and IP video inputs into high-quality outputs for broadcast and streaming workflows. This connector brings the Titan Live into DataMiner, so you can see the health of the device and of every service it delivers, and take action directly from DataMiner when something needs your attention.

## Key Features

- **Service-level visibility**: See every encoding service at a glance, including its status, streaming state, input source, and output destination, so you always know what is on air.

- **Full stream insight**: Look into the structure of each service, including the video, audio, and data streams it carries, with details such as codec, bitrate, and frame rate.

- **Service control**: Start, stop, or delete a single service or all services, and switch the active input or output, without leaving DataMiner.

- **Guided service management**: Adjust the settings of a service through a simple guided wizard, which reduces the risk of configuration mistakes.

- **Hardware health monitoring**: Keep track of temperatures, power supplies, fans, and core system services, so you can act on hardware issues before they affect your services.

- **Individual service views**: Represent each service as its own element, so teams can monitor, alarm, and report on the services they are responsible for.

- **End-to-end signal path**: Show how inputs are connected to outputs, which makes it easier to follow a signal through your chain and to locate where a problem occurs.

## Use Cases

### Keeping Live Channels On Air

**Challenge**: Live channels cannot afford downtime. Operators need to know immediately when an input is lost or a service stops streaming, and they need to act fast.

**Solution**: The connector continuously reports the state of each service and its inputs. When something goes wrong, operators can restart the service or switch to another input right from DataMiner.

**Benefit**: Issues are detected sooner and resolved faster, which reduces on-air interruptions.

### Giving Each Team Its Own View

**Challenge**: A single encoder often delivers services for several channels or customers, and each team only cares about its own services.

**Solution**: Each service can be presented as a separate element in DataMiner, with its own status, alarms, and reporting.

**Benefit**: Teams focus on what matters to them, and responsibilities are clearer.

### Tracing Problems Through the Signal Chain

**Challenge**: When a channel has an issue, it is not always clear whether the cause is the source, the encoder, or the output.

**Solution**: The connector shows how each input is linked to its outputs and displays the status of the streams in between.

**Benefit**: Troubleshooting is faster because the likely cause can be pinpointed without checking each device separately.
