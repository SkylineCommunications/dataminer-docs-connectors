# Inrow Fiberhome

The Inrow Fiberhome connector monitors and controls an Inrow Fiberhome in-row cooling unit over SNMP. It exposes operating state, temperature and humidity readings, equipment alarms, and status tables for the fan, system, outdoor unit, cabinet air supply, pumps, and electricity consumption.

Key capabilities include:

- Monitoring indoor and supply/return air temperature and humidity, operating modes, run times, and supply voltage.
- Monitoring fan, sensor, outdoor-unit, cabinet, and pump fault/status indicators.
- Viewing system, fan, outdoor-unit, cabinet air-supply, pump-cabinet, and electricity tables.
- Reading and setting supported temperature and humidity targets and device/group operating settings through SNMP.

The protocol identifies SNMPv2 as its communication type. Supported hardware models and firmware versions are not specified in the connector definition.
